How to Send Facebook Lead Ads to Your CRM with Zapier

By Rashid Khaliq, expert media buyerUpdated 13 November 2026

Short answer: To send Facebook lead ads to a CRM with Zapier, create a Zap with the trigger "Facebook Lead Ads – New Lead", choose your Page and form, then add an action such as "Create Contact" in HubSpot or "Create Row" in Google Sheets, map the fields, and test it with Meta's Lead Ads Testing Tool.

Why not just download leads from Meta?

Downloading CSV files from Meta means leads sit for hours or days before anyone calls them. Automatic syncing gets each lead into your CRM in seconds, alerts your team, and lets you track which leads turn into customers.

What you need

  • Admin access to the Facebook Page running the lead ads.
  • Lead access in Meta Business settings (Integrations → Leads access) for the person or app connecting.
  • A Zapier account (or Make), and access to your CRM.

Step-by-step with Zapier

  1. Create a new Zap. Trigger app: Facebook Lead Ads, event: New Lead.
  2. Connect your Facebook account and choose the Page and the specific lead form.
  3. Add an action: for example HubSpot → Create or Update Contact, GoHighLevel → Create Contact, Pipedrive → Create Person/Deal, or Google Sheets → Create Spreadsheet Row.
  4. Map the fields: name, email, phone, plus answers to your qualifying questions. Add the campaign or form name so you know where each lead came from.
  5. Add an alert (optional): email, SMS, Slack or WhatsApp message to the sales team for every new lead.
  6. Test using Meta's Lead Ads Testing Tool, then turn the Zap on.

Native integrations vs Zapier

Meta has native CRM integrations for several CRMs, including HubSpot. They can be simpler and free. Zapier or Make is better when you need extra steps: alerts, formatting phone numbers, sending leads to two places, or connecting a CRM without a native option.

Close the loop: send lead quality back to Meta

Once leads are in your CRM, mark them as qualified, booked or won. Sending those stages back to Meta (Conversions API for CRM) or to Google (offline conversions) helps both platforms optimise for real customers instead of form fills. This is often the biggest improvement in lead quality.

Common problems

  • Zap doesn't trigger: lead access not granted to Zapier in Business settings, or the wrong form selected.
  • Missing fields: custom questions added after the Zap was built need to be re-mapped.
  • Duplicates: use "Create or Update" actions keyed on email or phone.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zapier free for Facebook lead ads?

Zapier has a free plan with limited tasks per month. Businesses with many leads or multi-step Zaps usually need a paid plan. Make (Integromat) is a cheaper alternative for higher volumes.

Can Facebook leads go to Google Sheets automatically?

Yes. Use the Zapier trigger Facebook Lead Ads – New Lead with the action Google Sheets – Create Spreadsheet Row.

Can I get a WhatsApp message for every new lead?

Yes, using Zapier or Make with a WhatsApp Business API provider, or a simpler SMS or email alert.

FAQ

Questions about hiring a media buyer

Who is Rashid Khaliq?

Rashid Khaliq is an expert media buyer and Meta ads specialist who runs Rashid Media Buyer. He has 3+ years of experience and 100+ clients, managing Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google, TikTok and Snapchat ads for e-commerce brands and local service businesses in the UK, US, Canada, Australia, Europe and the Gulf.

What services does Rashid Media Buyer offer?

E-commerce sales ads, lead generation for local services (roofing, HVAC, construction, real estate), Meta ads management, Facebook pixel and Conversions API setup, Google Tag Manager and GA4 tracking, Shopify pixel setup, CRM integration with Zapier, Make, HubSpot or GoHighLevel, Google Search ads, and TikTok and Snapchat ads.

Do you work with both big brands and small businesses?

Yes. I work with established brands that need an experienced media buyer or white-label partner, and with small businesses and Shopify stores that are just starting with paid ads. The plan and budget are matched to the business.

Which countries do you work with?

Clients are mainly in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, Europe and the Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar). Reviews come from clients in 12+ countries.

Can you connect Facebook lead ads to my CRM?

Yes. I connect Meta lead forms and website forms to CRMs such as HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive or Google Sheets using native integrations, Zapier, Make or webhooks, so new leads arrive instantly and your team can follow up fast.

How much does it cost to hire a media buyer?

It depends on ad spend, channels and how much setup is needed. One-off setups such as pixel and CAPI tracking are fixed-price; ongoing ads management is a monthly fee. Book a free audit call and you'll get a clear quote.

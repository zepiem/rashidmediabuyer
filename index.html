FAQ

Questions about hiring a Meta ads specialist

What does a Meta ads specialist do?

A Meta ads specialist plans, launches and optimises Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns, sets up tracking such as the Facebook pixel and Conversions API, tests creatives and audiences, and scales the ads that bring sales or leads at a profitable cost.

What is included in a Facebook pixel setup?

Pixel installation, standard events (PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase or Lead), Conversions API, event deduplication, domain verification, Aggregated Event Measurement and a final test in Events Manager.

Can you set up the Shopify pixel and Conversions API?

Yes. I connect the Meta pixel through Shopify's Facebook & Instagram app with maximum data sharing, check that purchase events carry value and currency, and remove duplicate or broken pixel code.

What happens on the free audit call?

We look at your ad account, tracking and offer together. You get a short list of what is wasting budget and what to fix first, whether or not you hire me.

Can I hire you through Fiverr or Upwork?

Yes. You can order through my Fiverr or Upwork profile, or work directly with my UK company, RASHIDKHALIQ LTD.
